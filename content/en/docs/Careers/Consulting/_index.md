## Professional Consulting

The art of charging money to tell people things they probably already know, but with better frameworks and more confidence.

### Topics Covered

- **Consulting Frameworks** - Structured approaches to problem-solving
- **Client Management** - Building relationships and managing expectations
- **Project Delivery** - Scoping, executing, and delivering value
- **Communication** - Presentations, documentation, and stakeholder management
- **Business Development** - Finding clients and growing your practice
- **Specializations** - Technology, strategy, operations, and niche consulting

From independent consulting to working at firms, this section covers professional consulting skills and approaches.
