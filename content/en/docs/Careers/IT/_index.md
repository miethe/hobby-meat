## IT & Solution Engineering

Where technical expertise meets business requirements, usually with compromises on both sides.

### Topics Covered

- **Solution Architecture** - Designing enterprise technology solutions
- **Technology Evaluation** - Assessing tools, platforms, and vendors
- **Enterprise Platforms** - OpenShift, Kubernetes, cloud, and infrastructure
- **Integration** - Making different systems work together
- **Certifications** - Professional credentials and continuous learning
- **Career Paths** - Growing from engineer to architect to leader

This content draws from real enterprise experience - the good decisions, the regrettable ones, and everything learned along the way.
