## Pool Ownership & Maintenance

A pool is a hole in the ground into which you pour money and chemicals. But it's also great for summer.

### Topics Covered

- **Pool Maintenance** - Chemistry, cleaning, and keeping water clear
- **Equipment** - Pumps, filters, heaters, and automation
- **Troubleshooting** - Common problems and solutions
- **Seasonal Care** - Opening, closing, and winterization
- **Upgrades** - Lighting, automation, and improvements
- **Costs** - The real cost of pool ownership

From maintaining crystal-clear water to automating your pool system, find practical guidance from actual pool ownership experience.
