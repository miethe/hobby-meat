## Welcome to Outdoors

Sometimes hobbies involve fresh air and sunshine. This section covers outdoor activities and the gear that goes with them.

### What You'll Find Here

- **Beach** - Gear, activities, and making the most of coastal living
- **Boating** - Boats, maintenance, safety, and life on the water
- **Pools** - Pool maintenance, equipment, and backyard aquatics
- **Camping & Hiking** - Outdoor adventure gear and destinations
- **Water Sports** - Surfing, paddleboarding, kayaking, and more

These are the hobbies you tell people about at parties (unlike explaining your homelab setup). Research here ensures you spend more time enjoying the outdoors and less time dealing with equipment failures.
