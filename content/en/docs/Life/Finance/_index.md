## Personal Finance & Investing

Managing money so you can afford all these hobbies. This section covers practical financial strategies and investment research.

### Topics Covered

- **Budgeting** - Tracking expenses and allocating funds (including hobby budgets)
- **Investing** - Stocks, index funds, retirement accounts, and long-term wealth building
- **Savings Strategies** - Emergency funds, sinking funds, and financial goals
- **Tools & Apps** - Software and systems for money management
- **Financial Independence** - FIRE concepts and strategies

The goal is simple: make smart financial decisions so hobby spending doesn't require spousal approval (or at least can be justified with spreadsheets).
