## Culinary Pursuits

What starts as "I should cook more" inevitably leads to specialized equipment, ingredient sourcing, and strong opinions about proper technique.

- **Coffee** - Espresso, pour-over, and the endless quest for the perfect cup
- **Cocktails** - Home bartending, recipes, and technique
- **Equipment** - Kitchen tools, appliances, and when to invest in quality

From basic meal prep to advanced techniques, this section covers the research that goes into eating and drinking well.
