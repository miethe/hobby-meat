## Weather Monitoring & Meteorology

Running your own weather station means you can be wrong about the forecast just like the professionals.

### Topics Covered

- **Weather Stations** - Personal weather station selection and setup
- **Sensors** - Temperature, humidity, rainfall, wind, and atmospheric pressure
- **Data Collection** - Recording and analyzing weather data
- **Integration** - Weather Underground, CWOP, and sharing your data
- **Forecasting** - Understanding weather patterns and prediction
- **Storm Watching** - Tracking severe weather safely

From basic backyard weather monitoring to contributing data to meteorological networks, this section covers amateur weather science.
