## Amateur Astronomy

There's something humbling about pointing expensive optics at distant balls of gas while standing in your driveway at 2 AM.

### Topics Covered

- **Telescope Selection** - Refractors, reflectors, and choosing the right scope
- **Aperture & Optics** - Understanding the physics of light gathering
- **Mounts & Tracking** - Alt-az, equatorial, and computerized tracking
- **Astrophotography** - Capturing images of celestial objects
- **Observing** - Finding targets, seeing conditions, and dark sky sites
- **Accessories** - Eyepieces, filters, and essential gear

From first telescope purchases to advanced astrophotography, find guidance grounded in actual observing experience (and many cloudy nights).
