## Welcome to Science & Technology

This section bridges the gap between casual interest and serious technical implementation. From backyard astronomy to enterprise-grade home infrastructure, these are hobbies that require understanding the actual science.

### What You'll Find Here

- **Tech** - Homelabs, servers, networking, and running production infrastructure at home
- **Smart Home** - Home automation, IoT devices, and making your house smarter than you
- **Telescopes** - Astronomy equipment, astrophotography, and exploring the cosmos
- **Weather** - Personal weather stations, meteorology, and atmospheric science
- **Electronics** - Circuit design, embedded systems, and hardware hacking

These hobbies start with "I wonder how that works" and end with rack-mounted servers in the closet or a telescope that costs more than your car.
