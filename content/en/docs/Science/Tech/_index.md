## Home Technology & Infrastructure

Running enterprise-grade infrastructure at home is technically overkill. But so is having a dozen hobbies.

### Topics Covered

- **Homelab** - Servers, storage, networking, and self-hosted services
- **Smart Home** - Home automation, IoT devices, and integration platforms
- **Networking** - Routers, switches, VLANs, and home network design
- **Self-Hosting** - Running your own services instead of cloud subscriptions
- **Security** - Network security, VPNs, and protecting your infrastructure
- **Hardware** - Server builds, NAS systems, and infrastructure equipment

From simple smart bulbs to rack-mounted servers with proper redundancy, this section covers the spectrum of home technology implementation.
