## Modern Board Gaming

The golden age of board games offers incredible depth beyond Monopoly and Risk. This section covers the vast landscape of modern tabletop gaming.

### Topics Covered

- **Game Recommendations** - Curated selections by player count, weight, and theme
- **Strategy Guides** - Winning strategies for popular titles
- **Collections** - Building and organizing a game collection
- **Game Nights** - Hosting and community building
- **Solo Gaming** - Great games for one player

Whether you're looking for quick party games, deep strategy experiences, or epic campaign adventures, find recommendations based on actual play experience (and shelf space regret).
