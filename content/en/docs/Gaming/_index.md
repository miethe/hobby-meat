## Welcome to Gaming

Whether you prefer dice and cardboard or pixels and framerates, this section covers the wide world of gaming - both analog and digital.

### What You'll Find Here

- **Board Games** - Reviews, recommendations, and strategy guides for tabletop gaming
- **Video Games** - PC, console, and mobile gaming coverage
- **Game Recommendations** - Curated lists by genre, player count, and complexity
- **Setup Guides** - Optimal configurations for gaming systems
- **Community & Culture** - Gaming groups, conventions, and the social side of play

Gaming is one of those hobbies that can consume far more time than intended. At least here, that time spent translates into recommendations to help you find your next favorite game.
