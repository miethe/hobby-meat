## Photography & Image Craft

What starts as "I should take better pictures" becomes a deep dive into sensor sizes, focal lengths, and whether you really need that f/1.4 lens.

### Topics Covered

- **Camera Bodies** - DSLRs, mirrorless, and choosing the right system
- **Lenses** - Focal lengths, apertures, and building a lens collection
- **Technique** - Composition, exposure, lighting, and capturing great images
- **Post-Processing** - Lightroom, Photoshop, and digital workflow
- **Gear** - Tripods, filters, bags, and essential accessories
- **Specializations** - Landscape, portrait, macro, and other photography types

From smartphone upgrades to full-frame systems, this section covers the journey into serious photography.
