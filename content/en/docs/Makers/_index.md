## Welcome to Makers

This section celebrates the joy of making things with your hands - whether that's printing plastic widgets, building furniture, capturing images, or any other creative endeavor.

### What You'll Find Here

- **3D Printing** - Printer recommendations, filament guides, modeling tips, and project ideas
- **Woodworking** - Tools, techniques, and projects from beginner to advanced
- **Photography** - Gear reviews, technique guides, and post-processing workflows
- **DIY Projects** - Home improvement, repairs, and custom builds
- **Electronics** - Arduino, Raspberry Pi, and maker electronics
- **Crafting** - Various other hands-on creative projects

The maker mindset is all about asking "Can I build that?" before asking "Should I build that?" This section documents both the successes and the learning experiences (read: expensive failures) from years of making.
