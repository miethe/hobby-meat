## 3D Printing & Additive Manufacturing

What starts as "I'll just print a few useful things" inevitably becomes a collection of printers, filament types, and random calibration cubes.

### Topics Covered

- **Printer Reviews** - FDM, resin, and specialty printers across price ranges
- **Filament Guide** - PLA, PETG, ABS, TPU, and specialty materials
- **Printing Tips** - Bed adhesion, layer adhesion, troubleshooting common issues
- **Modeling & Design** - CAD software, mesh repair, and design for 3D printing
- **Project Ideas** - Practical prints and creative applications
- **Upgrades & Mods** - Improving your printer's capabilities

From first printer setup to multi-material advanced techniques, this section covers the journey of additive manufacturing at home.
