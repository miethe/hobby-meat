## Woodworking & Furniture Making

The satisfying combination of precision, craftsmanship, and making sawdust. Lots of sawdust.

### Topics Covered

- **Tools** - Hand tools, power tools, and outfitting a workshop
- **Techniques** - Joinery, finishing, and fundamental skills
- **Projects** - From beginner builds to advanced furniture
- **Wood Selection** - Understanding species, grain, and material choice
- **Workshop Setup** - Space planning, dust collection, and organization
- **Safety** - Protecting your fingers (they're useful for other hobbies too)

Whether you're building your first cutting board or attempting dovetails, find practical guidance from hands-on experience.
