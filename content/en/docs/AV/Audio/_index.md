## Audio Equipment & Hi-Fi

The journey into high-fidelity audio starts innocently enough with "I just want better sound" and ends with spreadsheets comparing THD+N measurements between amplifiers.

### Topics Covered

- **Speakers** - Bookshelf, floor-standing, and powered speakers across all price ranges
- **Headphones** - Over-ear, in-ear, open-back, closed-back, and everything in between
- **Amplifiers** - Integrated amps, power amps, and driving your speakers properly
- **DACs & Sources** - Digital-to-analog conversion and source components
- **Cables & Accessories** - Practical recommendations without the snake oil

From budget-friendly setups to diminishing-returns territory, find honest assessments and practical guidance.
