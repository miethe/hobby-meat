## Home Theater Systems

Building a proper home theater combines audio, video, room treatment, and enough cables to make you question your life choices.

### Topics Covered

- **System Design** - Planning and configuring complete home theater setups
- **Audio Systems** - Surround sound, Atmos, and speaker placement
- **Video** - Projectors, screens, and display options for home theater
- **Room Acoustics** - Sound treatment, absorption, and optimization
- **Seating & Furniture** - Theater seating and room layout
- **Automation** - Control systems and integration

From modest 5.1 setups to dedicated theater rooms, this section covers creating immersive home viewing experiences.
