## Display Technology

Once you learn what OLED burn-in looks like or understand local dimming zones, you can never unsee the imperfections. Welcome to the world of display technology.

### Topics Covered

- **Monitors** - Gaming, productivity, and color-accurate displays
- **TVs** - Home entertainment displays from budget to flagship
- **Projectors** - Big screen experiences at home
- **Technology Comparisons** - OLED vs LCD, refresh rates, HDR, and more
- **Calibration** - Getting accurate colors and optimal settings

Whether you're building a home theater, setting up a gaming battlestation, or just want to understand why some displays cost 10x others, you'll find technical deep-dives and practical recommendations.
