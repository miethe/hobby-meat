## Podcast Recommendations

The best way to continue learning while doing literally anything else. These are the podcasts worth subscribing to.

### Topics Covered

- **Technology** - Software, hardware, and tech industry insights
- **Science & Learning** - Educational content that doesn't feel like homework
- **Hobbies** - Specialized podcasts for specific interests
- **Business & Career** - Professional insights and career development
- **Entertainment** - Quality content for downtime

Recommendations focus on consistently high-quality content, good production values, and whether you'll still be subscribed six months from now.
